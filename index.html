Introduction

  • This chapter and the next togther Survey the scene in India from the end of the gupta era about 600 CE and to about 1200 CE

The Start

  • You are going back to 600 CE. Known as the 'colonial era', 'late classical' and 'early medival'
  • We will go on this adventure through the colonial era and finally stop at free India
  • We first start in north India. The mighty Gupta empire has fallen. You are a young traveller setting off on an adventure across the sub-continent.
  • Our first stop is in Kannauj, a city by River Ganga where a powerfull king hosts poets and scholars.
  • A weeks later you reach the chalukya kingdom in Deccan where you see magnicifent temples and lively cities.
  • In the far south the Pallavas are carving entire temples out of rock a Mamallapuram(also known as Mahabalipuram), While in bengal the Palas are supporting great universities like Vikramshila
  • At the time you wont see any large empires but you see strong kingdoms
  • As you travel you see something different - a land buzzing with new ideas, art and culture, different in each region yet still all part of one India
